## Facts (checked 2026-11-06)
- Hanoi, 1902: capital of French Indochina moved there that year. Governor-General Paul Doumer had built modern sewers, mainly for the French quarter; they became rat highways. (Wikipedia "Great Hanoi Rat Massacre"; Atlas Obscura)
- Third plague pandemic; rats were by then linked to plague. (Wikipedia)
- Hired Vietnamese hunters went into the sewers; paid per rat. Last week of April 1902: 7,985 rats; by June up to 10,000+/day. (Atlas Obscura; The Week)
- Bounty opened to everyone: 1 cent per rat, proof = the tail (officials didn't want piles of corpses). (Wikipedia; Atlas Obscura; Vann interview)
- Officials began seeing tailless rats; people cut tails and released rats to breed. Rat farms appeared outside the city; rats trafficked in from around Tonkin. (Wikipedia; Saigoneer/Vann; World History Connected)
- Doumer returned to France and became President of France in 1931. (Culture Trip)
