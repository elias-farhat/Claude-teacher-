## Facts (checked 2026-11-17)
- January 897, Basilica of St. John Lateran, Rome; Pope Stephen VI put the corpse of Pope Formosus (dead for months) on trial. (JSTOR Daily; pmamtraveller)
- Corpse dressed in robes, propped on the papal throne; a deacon appointed to answer for it. (JSTOR Daily; Wikipedia)
- Charge: usurping the papacy. Found guilty; all his acts annulled, including ordaining Stephen as bishop. (AV Club)
- Body later thrown into the Tiber. (AV Club; Heidelblog)
- The synod led to Stephen's downfall and death the same year (August 897). (Wikipedia)
