## Facts (checked 2026-11-18)
- James Edwin "Jumper" Wide, Cape Government Railways guard, known for jumping between railcars; fell and lost both legs below the knee. (Wikipedia; Amusing Planet)
- Made himself wooden legs and became signalman at Uitenhage station. (Amusing Planet; Medium/Jason Ward)
- Saw a chacma baboon leading an ox wagon at Uitenhage market (c. 1881), bought him, named him Jack. (Wikipedia; Mental Floss)
- Jack pushed his trolley and learned to work the signal levers; trains whistled a number of times for each track. (Mental Floss)
- A passenger complained; the railway tested Jack, he passed, and was employed: 20 cents a day + half a bottle of beer a week. (Mental Floss; VinePair)
- Worked 9 years, reportedly never made a mistake; died 1890. (Amusing Planet; Wikipedia)
