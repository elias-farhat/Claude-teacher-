## Facts (checked 2026-11-16)
- July 1807, after the Treaties of Tilsit; hunt organised by chief of staff Berthier on private land outside Paris. (The Star; Mental Floss)
- Berthier bought tame rabbits from farmers instead of trapping wild ones; unfed, they swarmed the hunters expecting food. (History Facts)
- Rabbits flanked the party; Napoleon retreated to his carriage; some rabbits leapt in; attack ended as the coach rolled away. (Mental Floss; The Star quoting Thiébault)
- Caveat: only detailed source is General Thiébault's memoirs (published 1890s); Thiébault disliked Berthier. (AOL; Just History Posts)
- "3,000 rabbits" is an estimate -> script says "hundreds".
