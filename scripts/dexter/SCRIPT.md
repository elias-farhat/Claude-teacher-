## Facts (checked 2026-11-16)
- Dexter (1747-1806), Newburyport, Massachusetts; poorly educated, mocked by merchants. (Britannica)
- Warming pans shipped to the West Indies; sold as ladles for molasses. (Britannica; Historic Mysteries)
- Wool mittens to the West Indies, bought by merchants bound for Siberia. (Historic Mysteries)
- Coal to Newcastle; arrived during a miners' strike and sold at a profit. (Britannica)
- Staged his own funeral to see who would mourn. (Britannica)
- Caveat: biographer W. C. Todd thought many stories were Dexter's own inventions -> script says "that's the story". (Amusing Planet)
