## Facts (checked 2026-11-16)
- Legend: in 1325 Modenese crept into Bologna and stole the oaken bucket from the town well; Bologna demanded it back, Modena refused, war. Fact-checks call the legend false. (The Star quickcheck; Amusing Planet)
- Real cause: Modena took Bologna's castle of Monteveglio. (Wikipedia, War of the Bucket)
- Battle of Zappolino, 15 Nov 1325: Bologna ~32,000 vs Modena ~7,000; Modena won; about two hours. (Wikipedia; The Star)
- Modenese chased the Bolognese to the city gates and came home with a bucket taken from a well outside Porta San Felice. (Wikipedia, Battle of Zappolino; Byline Times)
- Peace was made later; the bucket was never returned; a bucket is still kept in Modena (Torre della Ghirlandina / town hall replica). (Amusing Planet; Wikipedia)
